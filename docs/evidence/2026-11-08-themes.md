# Studio themes, depth effects, interaction feedback (D293 proposed)

Date: 2026-11-08. Branch ui/themes (rebased onto claude/alpha0-d1 after D292).

## What shipped

- Theme packs as data: `data/themes/<id>.json`, schema `data/themes/theme.schema.json`. Five packs: Violet Night (default), Neon Arcade, Studio Light, Tactical, Retro Wave. Embedded in the exe; files in `UJWALAM_THEMES_DIR`, `<exe>/themes`, `<app data>/themes` or the repo `data/themes` override or add themes.
- `theme.cpp` builds the stylesheet from tokens; live switch; `theme::color`, `theme::hex`, `theme::radius`, `theme::depth`.
- `depth.cpp`: one combined effect per widget (shadow, glow, extrusion), no blur pass.
- `interaction.cpp`: backdrop, caption type, popup fades. Hover, press and focus feedback in `depth.cpp` and `motion.cpp`.
- Settings > Appearance: theme cards, Reduce effects (`ui.effects`), motion (moved from Advanced). `ui.theme` stored too.

## Effect rules

- Shadow: floating things (cards, toasts, panels, menus).
- Glow: primary or active things only (record, live, primary button, checked).
- Extrusion: round controls and primary buttons.
- Reduce effects removes all three (flat). Motion off or reduce-motion: no animation, no idle animation.

## Repaint cost

Worst case: full 1600x900 window render, effect caches dropped before every paint, 100 repeats, offscreen. A single hover repaint is far cheaper.

## Limits and UNVERIFIED

- Shots are offscreen (Qt offscreen platform with `QT_QPA_FONTDIR`); real Windows platform rendering UNVERIFIED.
- Bahnschrift SemiCondensed picked by family name: UNVERIFIED (probe reports `appliedFamily`).
- 125 and 150 percent scaling UNVERIFIED.
- Real hover, press, focus animation UNVERIFIED (headless runs skip motion).
- Popup fade on a real display UNVERIFIED. Only fade-in on open exists for menus and dialogs; no dismiss animation (toast and panel exits stay).
- Some inline-styled widgets (task state labels, toast notes) may keep old colours after a live switch.
- Shadows and glow are clipped at the parent bounds.
