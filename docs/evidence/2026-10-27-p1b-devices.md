This behaviour is built into win-dshow and cannot be avoided while using it.

### Findings

- **Capture card: "No Signal".** The card opened and delivered 1280x720@60 NV12 frames, but the frames are the AVerMedia firmware "No Signal" screen. The card's HDMI audio was opened (44.1 kHz, resampled to 48 kHz) but was silent, consistent with no HDMI input. The phone was not delivering a signal at test time. Possible causes: cable or HDCP, or the phone's screen-mirroring state. UNVERIFIED which.
- **Insta360: 0x800705AA (ERROR_NO_SYSTEM_RESOURCES) at graph Run.** No known app was holding it: only Discord was running among camera-capable apps. Likely causes: USB bandwidth with two isochronous video devices running at once on the same controller, or the camera held by a background service. UNVERIFIED. The rules say no retry loop, so the camera-alone run was not attempted.
